# Representation, correspondence and conditioning motifs

Read when a module's contribution concerns canonical inputs, aligned domains, shared representations, latent distributions or multiple task specifications. These are optional explanatory moves, not stages of one model. Apply the established palette/type/geometry and the single-connector rules from their owners. Original-paper identities and screenshots stay in the project-local study.

## Canonical packing with actual availability

To explain differently ordered/sized inputs, show two short source rows, the actual index/coordinate mapping and matched canonical rows. Group cells by the project's actual types and place a corresponding presence row beneath them. One present zero and one missing/padded entry can make the distinction concrete. Show a separate root/anchor block only when its semantics differ.

Supply the index vocabulary, source mapping, coordinate frame, sign/scale normalization, padding rule and definition of presence. Matching packing does not establish shared weights or transferable commands. Presence, task-selection and attention-support masks are different operators. Keep exact index geometry regular; an opaque learned workspace does not acquire named anatomical roles merely because its cells are colored.

## Correspondence before a supervised mapping

Use two aligned temporal strips and one worked pair to explain why one domain supplies a valid label for another. Show the actual transformation into each domain and mark corresponding frames/windows with an association bracket. The label belongs to the paired observation/window, rather than an unexplained source-to-network arrow.

Supply pairing provenance, exact/approximate alignment, timestamps/rates, window support and label availability. Row alignment cannot create correspondence, and equal timestamps do not establish identical joints or dynamics. Independently captured semantic pairs need their actual window-level relation; they cannot borrow frame-level positives from a retargeted common ancestor. An association is distinct from a runtime computation.

## Alignment to a fixed coordinate anchor

Place the reference-map output and the new-map output in the same declared representation space. A few paired symbolic markers and short alignment associations can expose the objective; a local loss/update lane identifies the learned map. Put a frozen annotation on the actual fixed producer. Runtime can be a separate compact route from the new map to its consumer.

Supply the anchor, target producer, latent basis/scale, normalization/projection, objective and update recipient. Use a sphere only when the method projects there. Conceptual points are schematic; measured embeddings require actual projection/cohort data. A common representation does not imply identical policies, shared decoder weights or jointly changing coordinates.

## Distribution, sampling and retained execution

If a method is stochastic, distinguish its distribution from its sampled carrier. Actual condition inputs feed prior/posterior parameter operations; the sampling/reparameterization operation emits the latent into its consumer. A training boundary separates privileged/posterior inputs from the retained execution branch. Distribution matching stays separate from dataflow.

Supply family, parameters, conditioning, sample rule, matching objective and phase availability. A latent is not automatically Gaussian or variational. Addition of parameter offsets, addition of samples and mixture selection need different visible operators. A normalized representation sphere is not a probability density. Schematic density silhouettes need that label; numerical densities require real parameters.

## Representation algebra with an observable consequence

Two named carriers feed the actual weighted-combination/interpolation operator, followed by the resulting carrier and its real consumer. Display the coefficient beside the operator. A matched evidence strip can show the consequence if genuine evidence exists; otherwise show the symbolic interface only.

Supply a common basis/scale, algebra, coefficient domain and any normalization. Distinguish interpolation, extrapolation, action residual addition and reward combination. A line on a projected embedding does not compute high-dimensional algebra or establish smooth execution, feasibility or semantic composition.

## Different specifications, one downstream interface

Use aligned miniature branches with different input carriers and the actual conversion operation, ending in the same named interface. A target can be encoded, a temporal reference aggregated, or a reward/state cohort weighted; only the supported cases appear. Show the common consumer once.

Supply accepted specifications, conversion/aggregation rules, weights, time support, cohort and normalization. A goal, trajectory and reward function cannot all enter an unexplained prompt box. A discrete mode label does not establish free-form language understanding. Shared output shape does not establish equivalent behavior or zero-shot success.

## Generated representation, training target and execution consumer

Show the initial/noise carrier and actual conditions entering a generator, then name the representation it produces. A compact training side lane compares predicted and factual target quantities at the real objective. The execution route feeds the generated representation to its consumer, which may have its own state/observations. Expand the sampler only if its update is the focal operation.

Supply output type/horizon, prediction, update rule, projection, target producer, phase and downstream input contract. Generating latents, actions and reconstructed kinematics are different functions. A generator's supervision does not become a deployment input. A stage label alone does not establish freezing or gradient ownership.

## History, readout query and external condition

Preserve modality/time order in a history strip. Give the readout query a distinct carrier and expose the actual read direction with a local support example. An external specification enters its real conditioning operation separately. The updated query/readout and its projection produce the output.

Supply token ownership, available temporal support, attention visibility, projection/normalization and output rate. Observation history and future reference are different clocks. A readout query is not read-only memory. Repeated layers normally indicate depth; they do not imply iterative state updates or shared actor/critic parameters.
